# Baseline+ user guide

Baseline+ puts your training, sleep and how you felt on one screen, so you can see how they line up over weeks instead of trying to remember. It was built for active people living with a chronic illness, where a hard session or a bad night can show up days later.

These guides are for people using the app, including beta testers. Each one is short.

1. [Getting started](getting-started.md): creating your account, signing in, and finding your way around.
2. [Importing from Apple Health](importing-apple-health.md): getting your export off your iPhone and into Baseline+.
3. [The daily check-in](daily-check-in.md): recording energy, soreness and anything else you want to track.
4. [Reading your dashboard](reading-your-dashboard.md): what each part of the dashboard shows and where the numbers come from.
5. [Your data and privacy](your-data-and-privacy.md): what's stored, how to download it, and how to delete it.

Want a look before signing up? The sample dashboard (the **Explore a sample dashboard** link on the sign-in page) shows six weeks of made-up data.

## A note on health

Baseline+ shows you your own records. It isn't a medical device, and nothing in it is medical advice, a diagnosis or a treatment plan. When the dashboard says something like "Recovery day", that's a label for a number, not a recommendation. Talk to your doctor or care team about what's right for you, especially before changing your training or medication.

## Something not working?

During the beta, reply to your invite email or message the person who invited you. It helps to say what you were trying to do, what you expected, and what happened instead. Please don't send screenshots that show health data you'd rather keep private.
