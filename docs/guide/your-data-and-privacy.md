# Your data and privacy

## What Baseline+ stores

- Your account: email address, password (stored securely, never in plain text), and a linked Google account if you add one.
- Daily totals: steps, move, rest, breathwork, and a readiness score if your data has one.
- Activities that came with your data, such as runs or rides.
- Your check-ins and your check-in setup.

When you import from Apple Health, the export file itself stays on your device. Baseline+ reads it in your browser and only sends the daily totals.

Your records belong to your account only. Every page and download checks who you're signed in as, and nobody else's data is mixed in with yours. The sample dashboard uses made-up data and is separate from every account.

Baseline+ doesn't sell or share your data. There are no ads or analytics trackers in the app, and it doesn't keep logs of your health numbers or notes.

## Downloading your records

Go to **Settings > Your data** and choose **Download my records**. You'll get a JSON file with every saved day, activity and check-in (each answer, plus the fields you set up and what their scores mean), including days older than the dashboard shows. The Journal page has the same download at the bottom.

The file contains your health data, so keep it somewhere private.

## Deleting data

There are three levels, from smallest to biggest.

**One day.** In the **Journal**, pick the day. You can remove its totals, or clear its check-in, and each asks you to confirm.

**All of your data.** **Settings > Delete all data** removes every saved day, activity, Apple Health and spreadsheet import, and check-in. Your account, your sign-in and your check-in setup stay, so you can start fresh right away. It's useful after a test import that didn't go the way you wanted. You'll be asked to type DELETE first, and it can't be undone, so download your records first if you want a copy.

**Your whole account.** At the bottom of Settings, **Delete my account** removes your account, sign-in methods, records and check-ins, and signs you out everywhere. You'll need your current password and to type DELETE.

A few things deletion can't reach. Files you've already downloaded are yours to delete. And if Google sign-in was linked, you can also remove Baseline+'s access from your Google account settings.

## Backups

Baseline+ doesn't make its own backups of your data. The company that hosts the database keeps a short restore history of its own, so deleted data can stay there for a limited time before it's gone for good. Before the beta opens, this page will say exactly how long that is.

## Keeping your account safe

- Use a password you don't use anywhere else, at least 12 characters long.
- Sign out on shared computers with **Sign out** at the bottom of the sidebar.
- If you think someone else knows your password, reset it. That signs out every other device.
