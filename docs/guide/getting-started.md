# Getting started

## Creating your account

Baseline+ is invite-only during the beta. Your invite tells you which email address to sign up with, and the account has to use that exact address.

1. Open Baseline+ and choose to create an account.
2. Enter your email address and a password of at least 12 characters.
3. Check your inbox for a verification email and open the link in it. That signs you in and takes you to your dashboard.

If the email doesn't turn up after a few minutes, check your spam folder, then use **Resend verification email** on the sign-in page. You can ask for a new one once a minute.

## Signing in

Sign in with your email and password. If Google sign-in is offered, you can link your Google account in **Settings** once you've signed in with your password, as long as your Google account uses the same email address. After that, **Continue with Google** works too.

Forgot your password? Choose **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page and we'll email you a reset link. The link works for 30 minutes and only once. Resetting your password signs you out everywhere else, which is worth doing if you ever think someone else has your password.

To sign out, use **Sign out** at the bottom of the sidebar.

## Finding your way around

The sidebar has four places:

- **Dashboard** is your overview. See [Reading your dashboard](reading-your-dashboard.md).
- **Journal** is where you pick a day to fill in its check-in or fix its numbers by hand. See [The daily check-in](daily-check-in.md).
- **Import** brings in data from Apple Health or a spreadsheet. See [Importing from Apple Health](importing-apple-health.md).
- **Settings** is where you manage sign-in, set up your check-in, download your records, and delete data. See [Your data and privacy](your-data-and-privacy.md).

Baseline+ works in a phone browser as well as on a computer. The Apple Health import is easiest from a Mac, though.

## A good first week

1. Import your Apple Health history so the dashboard has something to compare against.
2. Look over the check-in fields in **Settings > Daily check-in**, and add any symptom you want to follow.
3. Do the check-in each day for a week or two. Comparisons need at least four recorded days in the past four weeks before they show up, so the dashboard gets more useful as you go.
